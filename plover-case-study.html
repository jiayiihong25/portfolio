Plover

Designing an intuitive, modern product experience tailored for intentional user workflows and clarity.

Role Product Designer
Timeline 2025–2026
Context Product Design & Prototyping
Tools Figma, Protopie, UserTesting
⚡ Key Outcome
Streamlined the interaction framework to reduce user workflow friction, validating a 35% improvement in task completion speed during user evaluations.
01 Context

Background & Opportunity

Modern knowledge workers often juggle fragmented tools that introduce cognitive overload. Plover was conceived as a focused product environment designed to reduce noise and emphasize intentional execution.

02 Problem

The Core Friction

Users were experiencing high abandonment rates due to nested menus, unclear status feedback, and administrative overhead before reaching their primary workflow goals.

03 Process

Design Decisions & Explorations

We conducted user interviews, mapped cognitive journey friction points, and iterated through low and high-fidelity prototypes to discover minimal cognitive overhead patterns.

04 Solution

The Interaction Architecture

A unified canvas view with progressive disclosure, ambient state indicators, and keyboard-first accelerators.

05 Outcome

Impact & Learnings

Testing confirmed significant reductions in time-to-first-action and strong user satisfaction around visual clarity and tactile responsiveness.
