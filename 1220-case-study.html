The Fritter Shop: Scaling a Local Favorite through Strategic Franchising

Determining the optimal regional growth and operational decentralization strategy for an authentic Dutch bakery.

Role Strategy Consultant & Designer
Award 1st Place (Ivey Business Competition)
Discipline Business Strategy & Storytelling
Timeline 2024
⚡ Key Outcome
Awarded 1st Place out of 400+ competitors with a 5-phase franchise scaling model, detailed unit economics, and operational knowledge transfer playbook for Ontario expansion.
01 Context

Background & Founder's Dilemma

Kelvin Van Rijn, founder of The Fritter Shop in London, Ontario, had captured the local market with his authentic Dutch fritters. However, growth had plateaued. He wanted to expand across Ontario but faced a classic founder bottleneck: constrained by personal operational time and capital without risking quality dilution.

02 Problem

Customer Journey Insights

1. The Sensory Experience: The warm bakery smell and in-person ritual were central to the brand. CPG licensing was ruled out as it destroyed the sensory magic.

2. The Founder Bottleneck: Kelvin personally handled quality assurance. Managing multiple corporate locations would cause immediate burnout.

3. Value of Local Ownership: Community-rooted franchisees were far better positioned to preserve local gem authenticity than distant corporate managers.

03 Process

Strategic Implementation Rigor

Rather than proposing high-level abstractions, we researched concrete real-estate storefront listings, exact renovation cost models, and unit-level cash flow projections across target cities (Waterloo, Toronto, Oakville).

04 Solution

The 5-Phase Franchise Playbook

Phase 1: Foundation & Vetting (Months 0–5) — Comprehensive franchisee screening matching cultural persona and operational capital.

Phase 2: The Build & Pilot (Months 5–7) — Site selection in Waterloo, matching London's demographic profile.

Phase 3: Knowledge Transfer (Months 7–9) — Codifying Kelvin’s tacit baking technique into standardized operational manuals.

Phase 4: Continuous Support Loop (Month 10+) — Monthly reviews and direct founder check-ins to safeguard product consistency.

Phase 5: High-Density Expansion — Reinvesting pilot cash flow into Toronto and Oakville flagship locations.

05 Outcome

First Place Finish & Strategy Learnings

Our strategy captured 1st Place out of 400+ participants. The key differentiator was coupling granular financial data with an empathetic human storytelling hook that resonated deeply with the judges.
